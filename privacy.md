# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

## Operator and purpose

Serhii Fedosov operates this application for personal, non-commercial finance tracking.

Contact: fedserfey@gmail.com

## Information processed

The application retrieves authorized account information through Enable Banking, including account identifiers, balances, transaction dates, amounts, currencies, merchant names, and payment descriptions.

It also stores API credentials and authorization-session information needed to retrieve transactions.

## Storage and access

Retrieved information is stored on my own computer and imported into my personal Actual Budget installation. Access is restricted to me.

Enable Banking and my bank process information as necessary to provide the authorized connection, under their own terms and privacy notices.

The application does not sell information or send transactions to AI services or messaging platforms.

## Retention and deletion

Transaction history is retained for personal financial analysis until I delete it. I can delete local records and revoke bank access. Revoking access stops future retrieval but does not automatically delete previously imported records.

## Changes

This policy will be updated if the application's data use, storage, or access changes.
