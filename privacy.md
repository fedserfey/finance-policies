# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

## Operator and purpose

Serhii Fedosov maintains these private household finance applications for personal, non-commercial use by him and his wife. This policy covers their separately authorized bank connections and the local household dashboard.

Contact: fedserfey@gmail.com

## Information processed

The application retrieves account information authorized by each participating account holder through Enable Banking and their bank. This may include account identifiers, balances, transaction dates, amounts, currencies, merchant names, and payment descriptions.

It also stores local account labels, categories, corrections, and the API credentials and authorization-session information needed to retrieve transactions.

## Storage and access

Bank snapshots, classification results, corrections, and generated spending reports are stored on the household's own computer. The current dashboard is a local report.

Access to household records is limited to the participating household members and the local software they authorize to process those records. Reports can combine spending from both participants while keeping account ownership distinct. Combining reports does not change who owns or authorizes each bank account.

Enable Banking and each participant's bank process information needed to provide the authorized connection under their own terms and privacy notices. The public website hosting these policies contains policy text, not bank records or credentials.

## Optional AI categorization

Selected transaction information has been processed in separately approved categorization experiments using TypeSafe (Jev) and OpenAI. Saved results may be reused in local reports without another provider request.

Any new use of an external AI service requires the relevant account holder's separate approval of the provider, information sent, and scope. Joining the household dashboard or accepting this policy does not itself authorize AI processing of that person's transactions.

An approved request may include selected payment descriptions, amounts, currencies, transaction direction, relevant bank codes, and reviewed categorization examples. Descriptions and notes are masked where practical, but masking may leave personal information in payment text. Bank login details, API private keys, and authorization tokens are not included in AI requests. Submitted information is handled under the chosen provider's terms and privacy controls.

The application does not sell information, publish financial records, or automatically send transactions through messaging platforms.

## Retention and deletion

Transaction history, raw imports, corrections, and reports are retained for household financial analysis until deleted. Each participant can revoke their own bank authorization and contact the operator to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their locally stored information.

Revoking bank access stops future retrieval under that authorization but does not automatically delete previously imported records or information already submitted to an external provider. External providers' retention and deletion arrangements apply to information they have received.

## Changes

This policy will be updated if the application's data use, storage, or access changes.
