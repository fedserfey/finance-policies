# Terms of Use

Last updated: 31 October 2026

## Purpose

These household finance applications are maintained by Serhii Fedosov for private, non-commercial use by him and his wife. They provide local spending reports and are not offered as a public financial service.

## Bank access

Each participant connects and authorizes their own bank accounts through Enable Banking and their bank. Accounts must be linked in accordance with Enable Banking's account-ownership requirements. Each participant remains responsible for their own bank approval and credentials.

Authorized household records may be combined in a shared local spending dashboard while retaining separate account identities. Shared reporting does not authorize one participant to connect someone else's account under their own identity.

The application does not initiate payments or transfers. Access can be revoked, and periodic reauthorization may be required.

## Optional categorization

Categories may come from saved suggestions or human corrections. AI suggestions can be wrong and are not automatically treated as confirmed classifications. New external AI processing requires the relevant account holder's separate approval, as described in the [privacy policy](privacy.html).

## Accuracy and availability

Bank data may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. Imported records should be checked against official bank statements.

## Ending use

Each participant can stop using the application and revoke their own bank access. Requests for a copy, correction, or deletion of locally stored information can be sent to the contact below. Revocation does not itself remove existing records or information already received by an external provider; see the [privacy policy](privacy.html).
