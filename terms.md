# Terms of Use

Last updated: 12 October 2026

## Purpose

This application is operated by Serhii Fedosov solely for personal, non-commercial finance tracking. It is not offered as a public service.

## Bank access

The application retrieves information only from my own accounts after authorization through Enable Banking and my bank.

It does not initiate payments or transfers. Access can be revoked, and periodic reauthorization may be required.

## Accuracy and availability

Bank data may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. Imported records should be checked against official bank statements.

## Ending use

I can stop using the application, revoke its bank access, and delete its stored information at any time.
