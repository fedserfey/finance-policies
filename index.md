# Household Finance Dashboard

A private, non-commercial household finance application maintained by Serhii Fedosov for use by him and his wife. Each participant authorizes access to their own bank accounts through Enable Banking and their bank.

The application stores account information locally and provides spending reports that can combine household transactions while keeping accounts distinct. It does not initiate payments or transfers.
